What is Carboon?

Carbon intelligence for early-stage design

CARBOON is a research-backed web tool that helps designers estimate and reduce the whole-life carbon of buildings. By linking parametric modelling with embodied-carbon analysis, it turns early decisions — massing, structure, and facade — into measurable carbon impact.

Instead of discovering a building's footprint after construction drawings are set, CARBOON shows the carbon consequences of form and facade choices while they are still cheap to change.

  • Concept-to-design embodied & whole-life carbon estimates
  • Form, structure and facade optimisation in one loop
  • Results mapped directly to LEED v5 credit pathways

Designed for designers

  • Parametric massing from Grasshopper
  • Evolutionary optimisation via Wallacei
  • LEED v5 compliance, built in
  • Clear, auditable carbon breakdowns
How It Works

Three steps from massing to LEED v5

A closed loop that connects generative geometry with carbon outcomes and certification.

Parametric Modelling

Drive building massing and facade options through Grasshopper so every form variant is generated and updated — not drawn by hand.

AI Optimisation

Evolutionary algorithms explore thousands of design variants with Wallacei and surface the ones that minimise embodied carbon while meeting performance goals.

LEED v5 Integration

Carbon results are translated into LEED v5 credits, showing the concrete path to Platinum through embodied-carbon cuts and renewable energy.
