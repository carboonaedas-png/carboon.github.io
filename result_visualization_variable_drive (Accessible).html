Where these EAc3 figures come from:
  • Point schedule: LEED v5 EAc3 — 5 pts at 5% EUI reduction, then +1 pt for every additional 1.5% (capped at 18 pts).
  • EUI reduction: (HK-norm baseline EUI − your glazing-adjusted EUI) ÷ baseline EUI. Baseline EUI is the editable HK-norm default (200 kWh/m²/yr).
  • Glazing → EUI: your loaded EUI is scaled by envelope OTTV. OTTV proxy = 8 + 22·WWR (W/m²), calibrated so ~55% glazing meets the HK commercial-tower cap of 20 W/m² (BD APP-67); only the cooling share (~45% of EUI) tracks OTTV, the rest is fixed load.
  • Sources: HK Building Energy Code OTTV caps (BD APP-67); LEED v5 BD+C draft EAc3 table; your project EUI/OC fed from the loaded JSON. These are concept-stage proxies for direction only — not a substitute for an ASHRAE 90.1 baseline model.

About the platform

What is CARBOON?

CARBOON is a whole-life carbon assessment platform that turns early massing-stage building data into clear, audit-ready lifecycle carbon intelligence — powered by your own AI form-optimisation research.

⚡
CARBOON connects building form, materials, and operational energy into a single EN 15978-compliant carbon picture — so design decisions can be defended before the building is detailed.
🏡

Whole-life, not just operational

Most tools stop at energy bills. CARBOON spans the entire lifecycle — embodied carbon from materials (A1–A5), operational energy (B), end-of-life (C), and beyond-system recycling benefits (D) — so you see where the carbon actually is.

For a typical mid-rise tower, embodied carbon can be 40–70% of lifetime emissions. Ignoring it hides the biggest lever your design controls.

🤖

Built for early design

CARBOON is made for the massing stage — when you still have form, structure, and glazing to play with. It reads the same JSON your Grasshopper / Honeybee workflow already produces.

No need to wait for a full MEP spec. Drop in geometry and a BoQ and get directional carbon guidance instantly.

📊

Visual & comparative

Interactive charts rank hotspots by lifecycle stage and building element, and the Materials Comparison lets you test structure swaps (RC vs timber vs steel) against a Hong Kong-norm baseline.

Why this exists

Why CARBOON matters

The building sector is responsible for roughly 37% of global energy-related carbon. Most of that is locked in before a single wall is drawn — which is exactly where CARBOON works.

🌍

Carbon is decided early

Up to 80% of a building's lifetime emissions are determined by choices made at concept and massing stage — form, orientation, structure, and glazing.

By the time a detailed model exists, the biggest carbon levers are already fixed. CARBOON gives you that window back.

🏡

Your AI research, made legible

You already optimise building form with AI for sunlight hours. CARBOON translates that form exploration into carbon language — turning a black-box optimisation into defensible, reviewable evidence.

That bridge is what makes "our AI helps us hit LEED targets" a claim you can actually stand behind.

🏢

Fit for Hong Kong & Taikoo Place

With OTTV caps, a RC-heavy norm, and dense mixed-use districts, Hong Kong needs local baselines. CARBOON bakes in HK-norm references so comparisons are meaningful, not generic.

For a LEED v4.1 Sustainable Community like Taikoo Place, form-driven streetscape and neighbour impacts matter — and CARBOON keeps that context visible.

💰

From insight to certification

CARBOON links directly to LEED v4 (EAc1/MRc) and surfaces LEED v5 credits (EAc3 operational, MRc2 materials) so research output maps to rating points.

It won't submit for you — but it makes the pathway from massing study to-certification transparent and traceable.

📚 Methodology guidebook
Under the hood

How CARBOON works

CARBOON takes the geometry and material take-off your design tools already produce, maps them to EN 15978 lifecycle stages, and renders the results as interactive, comparable dashboards.

1

Load your data

Upload a JSON from Grasshopper/Honeybee or paste it directly. CARBOON auto-detects project metadata, BoQ, and energy inputs.

2

Map to lifecycle stages

Materials are grouped into A1–A5 (product & construction), B (use), C (end-of-life), and D (beyond system) using ICE / IStructE carbon factors.

3

Visualise & compare

Charts rank carbon by stage and element; the 3D viewer shows your building form; Materials Comparison tests structural swaps.

4

Link to LEED

Operational and materials results surface LEED v4 and v5 credit points, with methodology notes for every estimate.

🧩

Data in

The dashboard reads a single JSON: building area, floors, WWR, structure & envelope type, a material BoQ, and — where available — EUI from your EnergyPlus / Ideal-Air run.

Your AI form study feeds the geometry; Honeybee/Ladybug feeds the energy. CARBOON joins them.

⚙

Engine

Carbon factors come from the ICE database and IStructE embodied-carbon guidance. Operational carbon uses grid emission factors for your chosen region/scenario.

Where a value is estimated rather than measured, it is flagged with an amber "estimated" badge — never silently presented as fact.

ⓘ
Two-run method: compare your AI-optimised form against a reference box of identical area and floors. The percentage drop in EUI is the form-attributable saving you can claim in LEED — no full MEP model required.

Get CARBOON

Download & set up CARBOON

CARBOON isn't a single compiled plugin — it's a Grasshopper workflow we packaged as individual script components. The form-finding rides on a couple of free environment plugins (Ladybug for climate/sun, Wallacei for the AI search), and the carbon maths itself ships as a Python script, carbon_engine.py, inside the downloads package. This page walks you through getting it running.

🔒 CARBOON v1.0 is ready

Grab the full CARBOON package below — it contains the .gh definition and the carbon_engine.py engine script — then follow the setup steps.

📥 Download Carboon v1.0
ⓘ
What CARBOON actually is: a set of Grasshopper scripts packaged into individual components (not a .gha installer). The core carbon calculation lives in a standalone Python file, carbon_engine.py — you paste its code into one of the components in the definition to make the tool run.

1 · Install the prerequisite plugins

Ladybug

Install Ladybug Tools (Food4Rhino or the package manager). CARBOON only uses Ladybug — for climate and sun-hour data that drives the form optimisation. The Honeybee, Dragonfly and EnergyPlus components that ship inside Ladybug Tools are not used by CARBOON.

Wallacei

Install Wallacei (evolutionary solver for Grasshopper) — this drives the AI form-optimisation loop (sun-hour objectives, generational search). Get it from Food4Rhino and drop the .gha into the Grasshopper Components folder.

Note: EnergyPlus is not required — CARBOON does its carbon math internally via carbon_engine.py, not through an energy simulation.

2 · Set up CARBOON

1

Install Rhino + Grasshopper

Make sure you have Rhino 7 or 8 (Windows) with Grasshopper. CARBOON is built and tested on this stack.

2

Install Ladybug + Wallacei

Install Ladybug Tools (only the Ladybug part is used) and Wallacei. Restart Rhino so the components register. Honeybee, Dragonfly and EnergyPlus are bundled but unused.

3

Download the CARBOON package

Download the package from this site. It contains the CARBOON .gh definition and the carbon_engine.py script — both are needed.

4

Paste the engine into the .csv component

Open the CARBOON definition in Grasshopper. Find the .csv file component we built, edit its code, and replace the existing code with the contents of carbon_engine.py. Save the definition — this is what makes the carbon calculation actually run.

5

Run it

Point the definition at your building and run. The carbon engine now executes from the pasted script and exports a result.json you can load in this dashboard.

⚙

Full dependency checklist

✓ Rhino 7 / 8 (Windows)
✓ Grasshopper 1.0+
✓ Ladybug (climate & solar — used)
✓ Wallacei (evolutionary optimisation)
✓ carbon_engine.py (CARBOON carbon engine — paste into the .csv file component)

🔗

Connect to the dashboard

The definition exports a result.json in the same schema this dashboard reads. Browse that file in the Dashboard tab and your whole-life carbon appears instantly.

The LEED v5 Potential tab reads MRc2 as calculated and EAc3 as passive-design potential — pending detailed operational modelling.

General Questions

Carboon is an AI-driven early-stage design tool that optimises building form and facade to reduce embodied carbon and support LEED v5 compliance. It was developed as part of the Aedas-HKU Research Internship Programme 2026.

Carboon is designed for architects, engineers, sustainability consultants, and developers who want to make carbon-conscious decisions during the early stages of building design.

Carboon is currently a research prototype developed through the Aedas-HKU Research Internship Programme. Please contact us for access and availability.

Technical Questions

You need a 3D building model (from Rhino or Grasshopper) exported as a JSON file. The dashboard will then analyse embodied carbon, operational energy, and LEED v5 potential.

Carboon uses material volumes from your building model combined with emission factors from the ICE Database and other industry-standard sources. It provides early-stage estimates, not formal LCA results.

Carboon provides indicative estimates for early-stage design exploration. For formal certification (e.g., LEED v5), you should use approved tools like One Click LCA, Tally, or EnergyPlus for detailed analysis.

Carboon provides potential assessments for MRc2 (Reduce Embodied Carbon, up to 6 points) and EAc3 (Enhanced Energy Efficiency, up to 10 points). It also tracks MRp2 (Carbon Assessment) as a prerequisite.

Workflow Questions
  1. Export your building model from Rhino / Grasshopper.
  2. Upload to Carboon.
  3. Review embodied carbon, operational energy, and LEED potential.
  4. Iterate and optimise your design.
  5. Use results to guide detailed modelling and formal certification.

Carboon integrates with Rhino / Grasshopper, Wallacei (optimisation), and can connect to EnergyPlus and One Click LCA for detailed analysis.

Glossary

Embodied Carbon
CO₂ emissions from material extraction, manufacturing, transport, and construction.
Operational Carbon
CO₂ emissions from building energy use (heating, cooling, lighting).
EUI (Energy Use Intensity)
Energy consumption per square metre per year (kWh/m²/yr).
OTTV (Overall Thermal Transfer Value)
Measure of building envelope heat gain.
LEED v5
Latest version of the LEED green building certification system.
